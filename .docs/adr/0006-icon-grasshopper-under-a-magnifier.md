# 0006 — Icon: a grasshopper under a magnifier

Date: 2026-10-27 · Status: accepted · Decided by: shrey

## Context

A plain magnifier is a common icon. Options drawn at 24 px: an L in the
lens, a tiny grasshopper, a jeweller's loupe, a wire with two nodes.
Shrey: "lets do option B instead i like the idea that you are trying to
look at a grasshopper".

## Decision

- The icon (toolbar button and library icon, `Resources/loupe_24.png`) is a
  magnifier with a green grasshopper in the lens, drawn in the outlined
  style of Grasshopper's toolbar icons.

## Consequences

- One 24 px PNG for now; a larger one is needed for food4Rhino and the
  Package Manager.
